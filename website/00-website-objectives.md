# Website Objectives

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines the objectives of HowToWriteYourMemoir.com.

Its purpose is to guide all future decisions regarding content, design, functionality, and website development. Every significant feature, page, and improvement should support one or more of these objectives.

---

# Primary Objective

Help people transform their life experiences into compelling memoirs.

The website should enable visitors to write better memoirs regardless of their writing experience.

---

# Secondary Objectives

## Build a Trusted Educational Resource

Provide practical, accurate, and comprehensive guidance covering every stage of memoir writing.

The goal is to become a trusted resource that people confidently recommend to others.

---

## Help Different Types of Visitors

The website should serve multiple audiences, including:

- people writing their own memoirs
- families preserving personal histories
- professional memoir writers
- ghostwriters
- memoir coaches
- editors
- publishers

---

## Support the Entire Memoir Journey

The website should help visitors throughout the complete process, including:

- deciding whether to write a memoir
- finding stories worth telling
- planning
- writing
- editing
- publishing
- marketing
- hiring professional assistance when appropriate

---

## Promote Related Products and Services

The website may promote products and services that help visitors achieve their goals, including:

- books
- courses
- memoir coaching
- ghostwriting
- editing
- publishing services

Promotional content should support the educational mission of the website.

---

## Demonstrate Best Practices

The website should be developed using well-documented design, content, and optimization practices.

The implementation process will be documented separately within the research repository.

---

# Success Criteria

The website should:

- genuinely help its visitors
- publish high-quality educational content
- continuously improve over time
- maintain a consistent user experience
- support the project's research activities

---

# Guiding Principle

Whenever a development decision is made, the following question should be asked:

> **Does this make the website more useful for someone who wants to write a memoir?**

If the answer is no, the change should be reconsidered.

---

# Scope

This document defines the objectives of the website.

Brand strategy, content strategy, technical implementation, and optimization are documented separately.
