# Experimental Design

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document describes how the study is conducted.

It defines the overall research design, the study subject, the observation period, and the relationship between website development, optimization activities, and data collection.

---

# Study Design

The project follows a longitudinal case study design.

A single production website is observed throughout its development.

Website improvements are implemented as part of normal development rather than according to a predefined experimental schedule.

Measurements are collected repeatedly over time to document how website visibility evolves.

---

# Study Subject

The subject of the study is:

**HowToWriteYourMemoir.com**

No comparison website or control group is included in the initial study.

---

# Observation Period

The observation period begins when systematic measurements are first collected.

The study continues throughout the active development of the website.

The observation period may be extended if additional data collection is considered beneficial.

---

# Development Process

The website is developed continuously.

Typical development activities may include:

- publishing new content
- updating existing content
- improving site structure
- modifying internal linking
- implementing technical improvements
- applying optimization activities

Each significant activity is recorded in the project change log.

---

# Measurement Schedule

Measurements are collected repeatedly throughout the project.

The frequency of measurements may vary depending on:

- website activity
- implementation schedule
- availability of measurement data
- research priorities

The measurement methodology is defined separately in **04-measurement-protocol.md**.

---

# Experimental Units

The study may evaluate observations at multiple levels, including:

- the entire website
- individual pages
- groups of related pages
- specific optimization activities

The level of analysis used for each observation will be documented whenever appropriate.

---

# Variables

The study records:

## Independent Variables

Documented website development and optimization activities.

## Dependent Variables

Measured website visibility metrics.

Additional recorded variables may be documented when relevant.

---

# Deviations

If the study design changes during the project, the changes will be documented together with:

- date
- description
- rationale
- expected impact

---

# Scope

This document describes the experimental design used for this project only.

It does not prescribe a general methodology for other studies.
